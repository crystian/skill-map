The session replay gained a director camera and a numbered trail: with the director on (transport-bar toggle, default on) the camera glides into a close-up of the node each frame is about, holds on frames about nothing on the map, and pulls back to the whole route at the end of the tape; every card on that route wears its step number, fading toward the start. The playback fold carries `trail`, and `spec/provider-activity.md` §Roles and boundary names the replay chrome as UI-owned.

## User-facing

Replaying a session now plays like a film: the camera follows each step up close and pulls back at the end to show the whole route, with step numbers on every node it visited. Turn the director camera off in the replay bar to keep the full route in view instead.
